About Sakīna

Authenticity

Every Qur'anic verse is presented in the standard Uthmānī script with translation from Sahih International. Every hadith is sourced from the canonical collections (Bukhārī, Muslim, Abū Dāwūd, Tirmidhī, Nasā'ī, Ibn Mājah) and the celebrated Forty Hadith of Imam an-Nawawī, with the traditional grade (ṣaḥīḥ / ḥasan) shown beside each.

No fabricated (mawḍūʿ) or weak (ḍaʿīf) narrations are included in this curated collection. Where scholars have differed in grading, the majority position is followed.

Typography

The Arabic is set in Amiri — a revival of the typeface used by the historic Bulaq Press of Cairo, designed to honour the classical naskh tradition. The English uses Cormorant Garamond and Cinzel for an editorial, manuscript-inspired feel.

How to use

Browse surahs by name, meaning, or revelation context. Filter hadiths by collection. Search is responsive — start typing in any field and the list narrows.

Read slowly. Sakīna means tranquility, the gentle stillness Allah sends down upon the hearts of believers (Qur'an 48:4). May your time here be a portion of it.
